# Fruit Farmer

Catch fruit in the orchard, sell it at the market, and grow your fruit farm.

**To play:** double-click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser. Progress is saved in that
browser on that computer.

## How to play

- **Groves:** tap Go harvest, then pick a grove on the village map. Each grove holds a set amount
  of fruit and refills over days. Fruit falls in a steady stream, with a quick shake now and then.
  Tap an arrow to scoot, hold it to zip, or drag your basket. A grove drops its own fruit, with a
  sparkly bonus fruit from your other trees now and then.
- **Market:** press on the fruit, drag down to pull back the slingshot, and let go. The dotted arc
  shows where your fruit will land. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to aim and Space to throw.
  Give each animal the fruit it asks for. The tag shows what the whole basket still pays, and
  animals farther back pay more per fruit. Missed fruit comes back to your tray.
- **Slingshot:** start your drag on the fruit. Drag above the fruit and let go to put it back.
  Tap a fruit in the tray to swap it in, and tap an animal to see who they are.
- **Farm book:** see your Farm Score, your totals for today, this week and this month, a diary of
  every day, and your farms. You can start a new farm and keep your colors and scenery.
- **Family:** in the Farm book, start a family or join one with a code to compare scores for today,
  this week, this month and all time. Farm names are made up by the game, and there's no chat.
- **Village:** a map you drag left and right, with buttons to jump between areas. Tap a tree to
  harvest, tap a friend's home to see them, and tap the barn to see what's inside. Sell fruit to
  animals to earn hearts, and at 3 hearts build them a home. Friends stay across every farm.
- **Stickers:** 70 stickers in the Farm book, including a Maker page for buildings, crops and orders. Fill a page to unlock a reward you can't buy.
- **New farms:** starting over earns Golden Seeds. Challenge farms pay double, and seeds buy rare
  animals, scenery and head starts in the shop.
- **Fruit Legend:** reach a Farm Score of 100,000 for a parade of all your friends.
- **Buildings:** build a Juice press, Smoothie stand or Jam kitchen in the village's kitchen row. Put
  fruit in and the goods are ready after your next harvest. Collect them to your barn, then sell them
  or use them for orders.
- **Fields:** once you grow three kinds of fruit, plots open in the village. Plant hay (ready tomorrow) or corn (ready in
  two days), then tap ripe plots to pick them into the barn. Buy more plots, up to 12.
- **Farm animals:** with fields open, build a chicken coop (corn makes eggs) and a cow barn (hay makes
  milk) in the barnyard.
- **More kitchens:** a popcorn cart, a salad bar (one of every fruit), a bakery (apple pie from apples,
  eggs and milk) and a diner (milkshakes and pancakes), each unlocked by the ones before.
- **Days:** the day shows in the village and while you harvest. Coming back from market starts the next day,
  or tap Sleep in the village to end the day whenever you like.
- **Farms:** the Farms tab in the Farm book lists every farm. Play any of them again, remove one, or start a
  new one. Start over from nothing (press and hold) wipes everything for a brand-new game.
- **Shop and Tool shed:** the Shop sells looks: basket looks and colors, scenery, friend hats and village
  decorations. Upgrades and a bigger barn are in the Tool shed in the barnyard. Plant new trees
  by tapping their sign in the groves; tap the barn to see what's inside. Golden Seeds have their own tab
  in the Farm book once you start a second farm.
- **Friends:** 18 animals arrive one by one as you collect hearts (the last six are a panda, a penguin, a
  raccoon, a mouse, a llama and a koala). Any friend with three hearts, rare ones too, can move into the village.
- **Finding your way:** the buttons down the left of the village (market, sleep, shop, Farm book, orders,
  friends) and the area buttons appear as your farm grows, and one-time tips point you to the barn, the
  Tool shed and other places.
- **Keep picking:** when your basket is full, one tap empties it into the barn and keeps picking the same
  tree (or the next tree with fruit). Every harvest ends in the village; go to the market from there.
- **Orders:** three friends post orders on the board in the village square, or tap Orders. Deliver
  from your barn for extra coins, hearts and farm points. Pin one and your harvest counts toward it.
- **Save code:** on the Farms page of the Farm book, get a code to back up your game and move it
  to another device.
- **Barn and levels:** fruit you catch goes to your barn, and you pick what to take to market.
  Every fruit caught or sold earns farm points, and each farm level makes your basket hold more.
- **Shop:** buy bigger baskets, new fruit trees, upgrades, basket colors and scenery. Reach the
  goals on the Barrel wall card to unlock a bigger market with 12 barrels.

## Changing the game

Every change is saved in Git. To see the game as it was before the new market:

```bash
git checkout before-new-market
```

Run `git checkout main` to come back.

Most numbers and content live in `js/data.js`. `CLAUDE.md` explains how the rest of the files
fit together. Claude Code reads it automatically when you open this folder.
